Before you start

StakeGuard is a bankroll and staking-plan calculator. It does not place bets, guarantee outcomes, or offer betting advice — it only tracks the math of a staking plan you define.

Betting carries real financial risk. Never stake money you can't afford to lose, and stop if betting stops being fun.

Multiple series is a Pro feature

The free version tracks one series at a time. StakeGuard Pro unlocks unlimited parallel series, so you can run separate plans per league, per day, or per bankroll.

Cost of a losing streak — percentage decay

Because every stake is a % of what's left, a losing streak shrinks your bankroll but mathematically never fully reaches zero — however, the math is asymmetric: lose 50%, and you need a 100% gain just to break even. This table shows what N straight losses actually does at your current %.
