StakeGuard

Complete User Guide

What this app is: a bankroll and staking-plan calculator with three independent staking strategies built in. It tracks the math of a staking plan you define — it does not place bets, connect to bookmakers, or guarantee any outcome.

Before anything else: betting carries real financial risk. Never stake money you can't afford to lose. If betting stops being fun, stop.

1. Getting Started (first time only)

  • Age gate & disclaimer — confirm you're 18+ and understand the app doesn't guarantee profit. Shows once per device.
  • 3-slide walkthrough — a quick tour of worst-case math, parallel series, and the auto-stop rule. Shows once; skip anytime.

2. Choosing a Strategy

StakeGuard has three completely independent staking strategies, switchable by tab at the top of the app. Each keeps its own series, settings, and history — nothing is shared between them.

3. Martingale + Progressive

STRATEGY 1

Stakes grow 15%-style on each win, and rebuild after a loss to recover it — with a hard stop after N consecutive losses.

SettingWhat it doesWatch out for
Odds (decimal)Payout odds you're betting atGet this exactly right — it drives every other number

Growth per win (%)How much stake increases after each winHigher growth = bigger exposure before a loss lands

Max consecutive lossesHard stop after this many losses in a rowRaising it needs a much bigger bankroll to stay safe
Recovery continuation modeReset to base, or pre-loss stake + growth% (smooth)Smooth mode means bigger exposure if another loss follows a hot streak

Lock-in floorLive (% of current bankroll) or Fixed (original base)Fixed mode ignores growth you've already earned

Important caveat: the bankroll requirement table assumes a loss streak starts fresh from base. If it starts mid-way through a growing win streak (before lock-in resets it), the real cost can be higher than shown. Tighter lock-in narrows this gap.

4. Flat Staking

STRATEGY 2

The same fixed stake, every single bet, win or lose. No growth, no recovery — the lowest-variance approach that exists.

SettingWhat it does
Stake (% of bankroll)Fixed to your original bankroll — never grows or shrinks with results
Stop after N losses in a row (0 = never)Optional discipline stop, since there's no built-in escalation to cap
Goal: total winsSame mechanism as Martingale — target and live progress

Because the stake never escalates, the cost of a losing streak is always exactly N × stake — a straight line, not a curve. No worst-case table needed; the math is that simple.

5. % Recalculation

STRATEGY 3

Your stake is always N% of whatever your bankroll is right now — recalculated fresh after every result. It shrinks automatically after losses and grows automatically after wins, with two independent safety stops.

SettingWhat it does
Stake (% of current bankroll)Recalculated live every bet — no fixed reference point
Stop if bankroll falls below X% of startingAn absolute floor protecting your original capital
Stop if bankroll falls more than X% below peakA trailing stop that locks in gains — rises automatically as your bankroll grows, so a hot streak's profit can't fully evaporate before the plan halts
Goal: total winsSame mechanism as the other two strategies

Both stops run at once; whichever triggers first stops the plan, and the banner tells you specifically which one fired.

The core math trap of this strategy: percentage losses and gains aren't symmetric. Lose 50% of your bankroll, and you need a 100% gain just to break even. The in-app "percentage decay" table shows exactly what N straight losses does at your chosen %.

6. Shared Features (Every Strategy)

Series

Each series is a fully independent staking plan within its strategy: its own settings, live progress, and history. Create multiple, switch between them by tapping a chip, rename by double-tapping, delete with the × (must keep at least one per strategy).

Dashboard (Pro)

Combined net P&L, overall win rate, and a per-series status table — rolled up across every series within that strategy. Each strategy has its own separate dashboard.

Continue vs Reset

Once a plan stops (loss cap, or a stop-loss threshold), you get two choices: Continue series resets only the triggering condition — bankroll, win/loss record, and full history stay intact. Reset is a full wipe back to your starting bankroll and zero wins.

Save as Template (Pro)

Save your current settings under a name, then pick it when creating a new series instead of retyping every field. Templates are separate per strategy.

Export Bet History (CSV) (Pro)

Downloads your full settings summary, P&L, and bet-by-bet log for the active series as a CSV file, openable in any spreadsheet app.

Everything Saves Automatically

Settings, live stake, streaks, P&L, and full bet logs save to your device after every action, for all three strategies independently. Nothing resets unless you tap Reset yourself.

8. Quick-Start Settings for a Novice

Martingale + Progressive: base stake 3–5% (not 10%+), recovery + profit target 0 to start, max consecutive losses 3, lock-in every 2–3 wins on Live floor mode.

Flat Staking: stake 2–5% of bankroll, optional stop after 5–8 losses in a row for discipline.

% Recalculation: stake 5–10% (15% is aggressive — 10 straight losses at 15% leaves ~20% of your bankroll), starting stop-loss 20%, peak drawdown stop 30–50%.

Whatever strategy you pick, confirm the relevant worst-case or decay table looks acceptable to you before placing a single real bet.

A Final Word

No setting, in any of the three strategies, changes the underlying truth: staking pattern controls how you win or lose, not whether your bets have real edge. If your actual win rate doesn't exceed what the odds imply, every strategy loses money over enough bets — some just show it to you slower or faster than others. If gambling ever stops feeling like something you're in control of, reach out to a national gambling helpline in your country — free, confidential support exists.
