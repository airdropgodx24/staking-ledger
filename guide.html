StakeGuard

Complete User Guide

What this app is: a bankroll and staking-plan calculator. It tracks the math of a staking plan you define — it does not place bets, connect to bookmakers, or guarantee any outcome.

Before anything else: betting carries real financial risk. Never stake money you can't afford to lose. If betting stops being fun, stop.

1. Getting Started (first time only)

  • Age gate & disclaimer — confirm you're 18+ and understand the app doesn't guarantee profit. Shows once per device.
  • 3-slide walkthrough — a quick tour of worst-case math, parallel series, and the auto-stop rule. Shows once; skip anytime.

2. Series — Running One or More Staking Plans

Each series is a fully independent staking plan: its own odds, bankroll, stake settings, live progress, and history.

AdvantageRun separate plans side by side — one per league, one per day, one per bankroll — without them interfering with each other.
LimitFree version tracks 1 series at a time. Adding a 2nd prompts a Pro unlock.
Actions+ New series to create · tap a chip to switch · double-tap a name to rename · tap × to delete (must keep at least one).

4. Bankroll Requirement Table — Know Your Worst Case

Shows what a full run of consecutive losses would cost, in two columns: Recovery only (breakeven) and Recovery + profit (your actual target). Green means covered; red means not. A live note shows your maximum safe base %.

Important caveat: this worst case assumes a loss streak starts fresh from base. If it starts mid-way through a growing win streak (before lock-in resets it), the real cost can be higher than shown. Tighter lock-in narrows this gap.

5. Live Simulator

  • Next stake — recalculated live
  • Odds for this bet — override the plan's default odds per bet
  • Win / Loss — record real outcomes; everything updates automatically
  • P&L panel — net profit/loss, peak bankroll, drawdown, record, win rate, streak/lock-in and goal progress
  • Continue series vs Reset — Continue resets stake progression only (bankroll, record, history stay intact); Reset is a full wipe
Be clear-eyed: Continue does not undo losses — it only resets the math going forward.

6. Everything Saves Automatically

Settings, live stake, streaks, P&L, and full bet log save to your device after every action. Nothing resets unless you tap Reset yourself.

8. Quick-Start Settings for a Novice

  • Base stake: 3–5% of bankroll (not 10%+)
  • Recovery + profit target: 0 to start
  • Max consecutive losses: 3
  • Lock-in: every 2–3 wins, floor mode Live
  • Goal: leave at 0 until comfortable

Then confirm both bankroll-requirement banners are green before placing a single real bet.

A Final Word

No setting changes the underlying math of martingale-style staking: it can control how you lose or grow, but it cannot guarantee you don't lose. If gambling ever stops feeling like something you're in control of, reach out to a national gambling helpline in your country — free, confidential support exists.
